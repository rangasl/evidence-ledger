    How a claim is judged

    The same four steps, every day.

    1. The case for

      One researcher builds the strongest honest case that the claim supports the thesis, starting from what the original study actually measured.

    2. The case against

      A second researcher, who never sees the first case, tries to kill the claim: failed replications, better explanations, measurement limits.

    3. The judgement

      A judge checks that every cited paper exists and says what we claim, corrects both sides, and rules. It also writes down what would change the verdict.

    4. The record

      The entry is published with every source linked. If new evidence arrives later, the verdict is revised in public, with the date and the reason.

    SurvivesThe strongest known attack fails. The claim stands as evidence.
    WoundedHolds only in a weaker form, which we state exactly.
    DeadThe attack succeeds. Not evidence for the thesis.
    UnfalsifiableNo observation could settle it. Philosophy, not evidence.
