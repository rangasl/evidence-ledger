The rerun

This small world has one inhabitant. It gets hungry, tired and bored, and it decides what to do about it. Read the script to see its next decisions before it makes them. Rewind, and it lives the same life again.

    Click the ground to drop an apple. The script rewrites itself, because you changed its world.

    Each thought bubble appears a moment after the decision is already in the script. That echoes what Libet reported in people, though Day 1 found that reading is weaker than it looks.

    This character is a program, so of course its life can be replayed. Whether the same is true of us is the real question. The determinism argument is claim FW08, and its verdict isn't in yet.
